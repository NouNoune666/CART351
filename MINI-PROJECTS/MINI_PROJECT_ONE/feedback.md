## *Face de book*, Kerven

- a log-in page, when you log in you realize you've been had because it hands you back your private info 

- super: it's very effective and simple, made me think of when I worked at Desjardins and they purposefully planted phishing scams like these and I fell victim to one and was embarrassing. the design, even though it's not the actual Facebook design like he wanted, is clean and appealing. 

- not so super: it was a pretty simple and fast experience 

- suggestion: revealing even more information on the client, turning it into an actual scam (jk)

## *My space*, Nerly

- a MySpace type website with a bunch of information to log in/sign up. that information is retained and showed on the next page. 

- super: the aesthetic and design is great, I love Nerly's style and it's always coherent. there's lots of information to give and so it feels like signing up for a real thing. 

- not so super: I think more could be done with the information so it's not simply shown on the next page 

- suggestion: seeing other people's information and being able to interact with it

## *Emotion Garden*, Winnie

- an online garden where you can plant a flower and assign it a feeling 

- super: I enjoy the meaning behind this project and also feel that "every feeling deserves a place to grow". I think the visual of a garden to represent data works great. 

- not so super: I think the flower's design could use some improvement so that they could be either more different or similar based on the distinct emotion 

- suggestion: having people with the same emotion be able to find one another or have a link. being able to design your own flower.
